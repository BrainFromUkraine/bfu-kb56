Firmware 1.0.1. The keyboard logic here is the same C code from src/app that runs on the STM32G030, compiled to WebAssembly. On the right, a host reads it over I2C the way an ESP32 would.

UA LED INT: 1, queue empty STM32RUN LanguageUA Capsoff Held—

MCU is in the ST bootloader

The host sent 20 B0 A5. The STM32 restarted into ROM at 0x1FFF0000: the application firmware is not running and 0x5E does not answer (NACK). An I2C flasher speaking ST AN4221 would now load new firmware.

Click the keys or type on your own keyboard: positions are physical, your PC layout does not matter. Fn is right Alt, UA/EN is \, Ctrl is mouse-only so the browser does not catch the shortcut. A click latches a modifier until the next key; a double click holds it until you click it again. Caps Lock: Fn, then Shift twice with a pause.
